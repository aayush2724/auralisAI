# Wisp Analytics — Customer Case Studies

*Internal sales enablement document. Approved for external sharing with prospects.*

## Case Study: Northlane Outfitters (Retail, Scale plan)

Northlane Outfitters, a mid-market outdoor apparel retailer, migrated from spreadsheets and a
homegrown tracking script to Wisp Analytics in March 2025. Within the first quarter:

- Checkout funnel drop-off was reduced from 68% to 54% after identifying a friction point at
  the shipping-address step using Wisp's funnel analysis
- The marketing team used the no-code segment builder to create a "cart abandoners, high
  intent" audience, which was synced directly to their ad platform and drove a 22% lift in
  retargeting conversion
- Time to build a new funnel report dropped from roughly 3 days (previously requiring a data
  analyst) to under 20 minutes

**Quote from their Head of Growth:** "We didn't need to hire a dedicated analyst just to answer
basic questions about where users were dropping off. That was the whole pitch, and it held up."

## Case Study: Bellcrest Health (Healthcare, Momentum Scale plan)

Bellcrest Health, a telehealth scheduling platform, adopted Wisp Analytics' Momentum Scale tier
in June 2025 specifically for the HIPAA-eligible infrastructure.

- Reduced patient no-show rate by 11% after using retention cohort analysis to identify which
  onboarding reminders correlated with completed appointments
- Compliance team completed their vendor security review in 9 days, citing the availability of
  the SOC 2 Type II report as the main accelerant
- Now uses real-time anomaly alerting to flag unexpected drops in appointment booking rate,
  which previously went unnoticed for up to two weeks

## Case Study: Fenwick & Ore (B2B SaaS, Scale plan)

Fenwick & Ore, a project management tool for construction firms, switched from Pathlytics to
Wisp Analytics in January 2025 after hitting Pathlytics' 13-month data retention limit during a
year-over-year churn analysis.

- Recovered 22 months of historical event data during onboarding via the Pathlytics migration
  import tool
- Used session replay to diagnose a confusing onboarding step that was silently causing 15% of
  new signups to abandon before activation
- Onboarding completed in 8 business days, ahead of the standard 10-day timeline, due to an
  existing Snowflake integration that simplified schema mapping
